Final design

Three rings

Three fused rings, one chain of records: each session is a link in the ledger.

  • Reads as a chain of links, which is what a ledger is, with no explanation needed
  • One union of three rings on a 26-unit grid, so it stays solid down to 16 px
  • Colours come from the site: ink, amber and, on light backgrounds, a deeper amber
